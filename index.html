02 — The bridal room

Signature brides, since 1988.

Annie Mansoor built her name on bridal makeup, and the Karachi salon keeps a dedicated makeup room for brides and party looks — skin, hair and photography under one roof.

How a bridal booking works

  1. Book your date. Call or WhatsApp to hold your day — bridal dates go early, especially in wedding season.
  2. Meet your artist. After your appointment is confirmed, the team maps your look to your dress, your skin and the event — the salon’s own makeup-mapping step.
  3. The big day. Hair, makeup and finishing touches in the dedicated makeup room, so you walk out camera-ready.

06 — The salon

A Karachi name since 1988.

Allenora Annie’s Signature Salon began in 1988 as the brainchild of Annie Mansoor, a stylist revered across Pakistan as a top bridal artist. What started as one chair has grown into a chain of more than twelve salons — and the Karachi branch on Ittehad Commercial carries the same signature: bridal makeup, hair, skin and spa, done by trained artists in a hygienic, professional setting.

The salon was among the first in Pakistan to offer brides everything under one roof — skin, hair and photography — and it still works that way today.

09 — Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes — book by calling 021 35824164 or WhatsApp 0345 8390444. Bridal bookings are by appointment, and dates go early in wedding season.

Do you do bridal makeup?

Yes. Signature bridal makeup is the salon’s hallmark, done in a dedicated makeup room, with hair, mehndi and photography available under the same roof.

Which facials do you offer?

Deep cleansing, herbal, glowing whitening, acne and pearl whitening facials, plus the chain’s signature HydraFacial and spa treatments.

Do you offer hair treatments like X-Tenso and keratin?

Yes — rebonding, X-Tenso, permanent keratin and conditioning treatments sit alongside cuts, colour, balayage and hair extensions.
